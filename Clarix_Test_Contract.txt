NON-DISCLOSURE AND SECRET KEEPING AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into on this day between "The Disclosing Party" (hereinafter referred to as "The Company") and "The Receiving Party" (hereinafter referred to as "The Employee").

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
The Employee agrees to never, under any circumstances, reveal the secret ingredient of the Company's famous chocolate chip cookies. The secret ingredient is defined as "a pinch of salt, a lot of butter, and a hint of magic."

2. NON-COMPETE CLAUSE
The Employee agrees that for a period of exactly 99 years after leaving The Company, they shall not bake, consume, or look at any chocolate chip cookies within a 500-mile radius of any known bakery. This clause is definitely enforceable in all 50 states and possibly on Mars.

3. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITIES
If The Employee accidentally drops a cookie on the floor, they must immediately indemnify The Company for the loss of potential deliciousness. Furthermore, The Employee agrees to waive their right to complain about burnt cookies. 

4. BIZARRE PROVISION
The Employee agrees to wear a silly hat every Tuesday. Failure to comply will result in immediate termination and the confiscation of all office supplies on The Employee's desk, especially the good pens.

5. SEVERABILITY
If any provision of this Agreement is found to be absolutely ridiculous by a court of law, the rest of the Agreement shall remain in full force, because we said so.
