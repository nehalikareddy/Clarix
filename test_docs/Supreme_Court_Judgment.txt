# SUPREME COURT OF INDIA
**Original Civil Jurisdiction**
**Case No:** Writ Petition (Civil) No. 123 of 2023

**Petitioner:** Major Bahadur Singh
**Respondent:** Union of India & Ors.
**Bench:** Hon’ble Chief Justice, Hon’ble Justice A.K. Kumar

## JUDGMENT
The court has reviewed the petition regarding the downgrading of the Annual Confidential Report (ACR). The petitioner argued that the uncommunicated downgrading affected his promotion.

### VERDICT
We find that the high court erred in directing a total review of the promotion board. Judicial review in military promotions is strictly limited. The assessment by the Selection Board is subjective and based on the entire service record. 

However, we note a highly concerning procedural lapse. The respondents are directed to reconsider the petitioner's case within 3 months. The petition is disposed of with no order as to costs.
