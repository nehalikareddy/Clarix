# RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Rent Agreement is made on this 1st day of January 2024, by and between:
**LANDLORD:** Mr. Ramesh Sharma, residing at 45 MG Road, Bangalore.
**TENANT:** Ms. Priya Patel, residing at 12 Park Street, Bangalore.

## 1. PROPERTY DETAILS
The Landlord agrees to rent out the property located at Flat 4B, Sunrise Apartments, Indiranagar, Bangalore to the Tenant.

## 2. RENT AND DEPOSIT
The monthly rent shall be ₹25,000 (Rupees Twenty-Five Thousand only), payable on or before the 5th of every month. The Tenant has paid a refundable security deposit of ₹1,000,000 (Rupees One Lakh only).

## 3. UNUSUAL CLAUSES
The Tenant shall not cook any food that smells like cabbage on alternate Tuesdays. The Tenant also agrees to forfeit the entire security deposit if a single scratch is found on the main door.

Signed by Landlord and Tenant.
